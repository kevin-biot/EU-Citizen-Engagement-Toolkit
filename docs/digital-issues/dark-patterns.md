# Dark Patterns And Manipulative Design

## Typical Problem

A website, app, or marketplace uses deceptive design to push consent, subscriptions, purchases, or account retention.

## Save First

- screen recordings if possible
- screenshots of each step
- the page URL
- date and time
- device and browser used
- any price, consent, or cancellation screens

## First Route

- complain directly to the trader or platform
- request cancellation, refund, or correction if relevant
- preserve the exact interface sequence

## Likely Public Routes

- national consumer authority
- data protection authority if the pattern concerns consent, cookies, or personal-data processing
- Digital Services Coordinator if the design is platform-related and falls into intermediary governance concerns

## EU Layer

This issue often sits between consumer law, GDPR-style consent rules, and platform regulation.

## Escalate When

- the business denies the interface was misleading
- cancellation or unsubscribe mechanisms are blocked
- a recurring pattern affects many users

## Useful Ask

Ask for:
- refund or reversal where relevant
- confirmation of consent records
- lawful basis for processing
- copy of cancellation flow and policy basis
