# Template Email: Online Scam, Fake Seller, Or Marketplace Failure

## Suggested Subject

Urgent complaint about seller, listing, or marketplace failure

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am reporting a scam, fake seller, or serious marketplace failure on [platform name].

Order or transaction reference: [reference]
Seller name and URL: [name and URL]
Listing URL: [URL]
Date of purchase or payment: [date]

Problem summary:
[non-delivery / fake trader / impersonation / refusal to refund / other]

I am requesting:
1. urgent review of the seller and listing
2. refund or reversal where applicable
3. confirmation of the trader identity information held by the platform, where lawfully available
4. a copy of the platform's decision and complaint route

Evidence preserved:
- order confirmation: [yes/no]
- payment proof: [yes/no]
- screenshots of listing: [yes/no]
- platform messages: [yes/no]

Please confirm receipt and the timeline for action.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
