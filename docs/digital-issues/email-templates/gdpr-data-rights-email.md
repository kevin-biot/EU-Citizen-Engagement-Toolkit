# Template Email: Data Access, Deletion, Or Profiling Problem

## Suggested Subject

Data rights request regarding [account or service]

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am exercising my data protection rights regarding [service or account name].

Identity or account reference: [account email / username / customer number]
Previous request date: [date, if this is a follow-up]

I am requesting:
1. access to my personal data held by your organisation
2. the purposes and legal basis for processing
3. recipients or categories of recipients
4. retention periods
5. meaningful information about profiling or automated decision-making, if applicable
[add deletion / correction / portability request if relevant]

If you believe you cannot comply fully, please explain the exact reason and the complaint route to the competent data protection authority.

Relevant context:
[brief factual summary]

Please confirm receipt and tell me the expected response date.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
