# Template Email: Illegal Content Or Notice-And-Action Problem

## Suggested Subject

Follow-up on illegal content report: [URL or reference number]

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am following up on a report I submitted about content or a listing on [platform name].

Reported URL: [URL]
Date and time reported: [date and time]
Reference number: [if available]
Reason reported: [brief legal or policy basis]

I am requesting:
1. confirmation that my report was received
2. the status of the review
3. the outcome and reasons for the decision
4. the internal complaint or escalation route if I disagree with the outcome

The content or listing remains harmful because:
[2-4 short factual lines]

Attached or linked evidence:
- screenshot before report: [yes/no]
- screenshot after report: [yes/no]
- copy of report text: [yes/no]

Please respond with the decision and next review route.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
