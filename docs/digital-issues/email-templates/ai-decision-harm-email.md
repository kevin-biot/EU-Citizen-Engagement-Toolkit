# Template Email: AI Decision Harm Or Opaque Automated Output

## Suggested Subject

Request for review and explanation of AI-supported decision

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am writing about a decision or output from [service or provider name] that appears to have been automated or AI-assisted.

Date of decision or output: [date and time]
Affected service or feature: [feature name]
Reference number or account ID: [if available]

The result was:
[brief factual description]

The impact on me was:
[brief factual description]

I am requesting:
1. confirmation of whether the decision was automated, AI-assisted, or human-made
2. the review route for challenging or correcting the result
3. the criteria, grounds, or record used for the decision where you can lawfully provide them
4. confirmation of whether a human can re-check the outcome
5. the contact point for a formal complaint

Evidence available:
- decision notice or output: [yes/no]
- screenshots: [yes/no]
- prompts or inputs: [yes/no]

Please confirm receipt and explain the next review step.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
