# Template Email: Platform Account Suspension Or Removal

## Suggested Subject

Account restriction review request for [account identifier]

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am writing about the restriction, suspension, or removal of my account/content on [platform name].

Account identifier: [username, account email, seller ID, or channel ID]
Date and time of action: [date and time]
Affected content or service: [brief description]

I am requesting:
1. the exact reason for the action
2. the specific policy or rule relied on
3. a copy of the statement of reasons or equivalent decision notice
4. confirmation of whether automation was used
5. confirmation of whether a human review is available or has already taken place
6. the route and deadline for challenging this decision

Relevant references:
- notice received: [quote or short summary]
- URL or content reference: [URL or identifier]
- appeal already filed: [yes/no, date, reference number]

Please confirm receipt and tell me what review route applies to this case.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
