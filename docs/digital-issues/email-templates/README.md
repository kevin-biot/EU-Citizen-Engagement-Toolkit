# Email Templates

Short citizen-facing email templates linked from the digital issue playbooks.

These are starting points, not legal documents. Replace the bracketed fields with the actual facts, keep the ask narrow, and attach or link only the evidence needed for the recipient to act.

## Current Templates

- [Platform account suspension or removal](./platform-account-suspension-email.md)
- [Illegal content or notice-and-action problem](./illegal-content-and-dsa-email.md)
- [Dark patterns and manipulative design](./dark-patterns-email.md)
- [Data access, deletion, or profiling problem](./gdpr-data-rights-email.md)
- [AI decision harm or opaque automated output](./ai-decision-harm-email.md)
- [Online scam, fake seller, or marketplace failure](./online-scam-and-marketplace-harm-email.md)
- [Inaccessible digital service or app](./digital-accessibility-failure-email.md)
- [Cross-border online consumer dispute](./cross-border-consumer-dispute-email.md)

## Use Them Like This

- send to the platform, trader, controller, or service operator first where appropriate
- keep one email focused on one concrete problem
- include dates, URLs, account or order identifiers, and the specific action you want
- preserve the sent email and any reply for later escalation
