# Template Email: Cross-Border Online Consumer Dispute

## Suggested Subject

Cross-border consumer complaint regarding [order or contract reference]

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am writing about a cross-border consumer dispute involving [trader or platform name].

Order or contract reference: [reference]
Date of purchase: [date]
Trader identity shown at purchase: [exact wording]
Trader country shown at purchase: [country]

The problem is:
[refund refusal / non-delivery / cancellation issue / trader identity problem / other]

I am requesting:
1. confirmation of the trader's legal identity
2. the basis on which my refund, cancellation, or complaint was rejected
3. the deadline for resolving this complaint
4. the internal escalation route if this is not resolved now

Evidence available:
- order confirmation: [yes/no]
- payment proof: [yes/no]
- terms shown at purchase: [yes/no]
- support correspondence: [yes/no]

Please confirm receipt and the next step for resolving this dispute.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
