# Template Email: Dark Patterns And Manipulative Design

## Suggested Subject

Complaint about misleading interface flow on [service name]

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am writing to complain about a misleading or manipulative interface flow on [service name].

Page or feature: [URL or feature name]
Date observed: [date and time]
Device and browser: [device and browser]

The interface problem is:
[brief factual description]

This affected me by:
[subscription / payment / consent / cancellation / account retention impact]

I am requesting:
1. correction of the interface or process
2. cancellation, refund, or reversal where relevant
3. confirmation of the consent or transaction record relied on
4. the contact route for a formal complaint if this is not resolved

Evidence preserved:
- screenshots: [yes/no]
- screen recording: [yes/no]
- pricing or consent screens: [yes/no]

Please confirm what corrective action you will take and on what timeline.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
