# Template Email: Inaccessible Digital Service Or App

## Suggested Subject

Accessibility failure report for [service or page]

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am reporting an accessibility problem affecting [service, app, or page name].

URL or feature: [URL or feature name]
Date and time observed: [date and time]
Device, browser, and assistive technology: [details]

The accessibility failure is:
[brief factual description]

This prevents or seriously limits:
[task or access blocked]

I am requesting:
1. an accessible alternative channel immediately
2. confirmation of the responsible accessibility contact
3. the expected timeline for remediation
4. the current accessibility statement, if one exists

Evidence preserved:
- screenshots or recording: [yes/no]
- page URL and app version: [yes/no]

Please confirm receipt and tell me what workaround or fix you can provide.

Regards,
[name]
[contact email]
```
