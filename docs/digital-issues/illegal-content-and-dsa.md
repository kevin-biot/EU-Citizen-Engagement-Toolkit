# Illegal Content Or Notice-And-Action Problem

## Typical Problem

You reported illegal or clearly unlawful content, product listings, or services and the platform ignored, mishandled, or obscured the process.

## Save First

- the URL of the reported content
- screenshots before and after reporting
- the report text you submitted
- any acknowledgement or reference number
- the platform response, if any
- legal basis you believe applies

## First Route

- use the platform's formal reporting route
- keep the exact wording of the report
- record whether the content stayed up, was removed, or was only partially acted on

## Likely Public Routes

- national Digital Services Coordinator
- sector regulator if the content concerns a regulated area
- consumer authority if the listing or service is commercial and misleading
- law enforcement route where the content is clearly criminal

## EU Layer

The Digital Services Act is the main EU process layer for notice-and-action duties, complaint handling, and transparency obligations for intermediary services.

## Escalate When

- the reporting route is unusable
- the platform gives no meaningful outcome
- the content is urgent or harmful
- similar reports show a pattern of systemic failure

## Useful Ask

Ask for:
- the platform's decision on the report
- timing of review
- internal complaint route
- whether trusted-flagger or escalation mechanisms exist for this issue type
