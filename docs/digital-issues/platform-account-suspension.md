# Platform Account Suspension Or Removal

## Typical Problem

A platform suspended, disabled, restricted, or removed your account, content, or monetisation access without a clear explanation.

## Save First

- the notice you received
- the exact text shown on-screen
- screenshots of the restriction
- your account identifier
- timestamps for the action and any appeal you filed
- the platform's policy page and help page
- any business or livelihood impact if relevant

## First Route

- use the platform's internal appeal or complaint route
- request the exact reason, policy basis, and review path
- save the appeal confirmation

## Likely Public Routes

- national Digital Services Coordinator if the issue concerns platform process, transparency, or notice-and-action compliance
- national consumer authority if the problem includes unfair commercial practice or misleading terms
- data protection authority if profiling or account decision-making relies on personal data in a way you want explained or challenged

## EU Layer

The Digital Services Act may matter if the platform is a covered intermediary and the issue is about complaint handling, transparency, statement of reasons, or trader/platform obligations.

## Escalate When

- the platform gives no reason
- the appeal route is unavailable or circular
- the account loss affects business continuity, speech, or repeated access
- a pattern affects many users

## Useful Ask

Ask for:
- the statement of reasons
- the exact policy provision invoked
- whether automation was used
- whether a human review took place
- how to challenge the outcome
