# Inaccessible Digital Service Or App

## Typical Problem

A public or essential digital service is not usable with assistive technology, keyboard navigation, captions, readable contrast, or accessible document formats.

## Save First

- page URLs and app version
- screenshots or screen recordings
- device, browser, and assistive technology used
- exact failure point
- date and time
- any accessibility statement published by the service

## First Route

- report the failure to the service operator
- cite the page, feature, and impact clearly
- ask for an accessible alternative and timeline for fix

## Likely Public Routes

- national accessibility monitoring or equality body
- ombuds route where the inaccessible service is public-sector and administratively unresponsive
- sector regulator if the digital service is tied to regulated communications or media functions

## EU Layer

Accessibility issues often run through national enforcement, but the legal framing may involve EU accessibility and public-sector digital obligations.

## Escalate When

- the service provides no accessible workaround
- the accessibility statement is missing or misleading
- the failure blocks education, health, benefits, mobility, or civic participation

## Useful Ask

Ask for:
- accessible alternative channel
- remediation timeline
- responsible accessibility contact
- copy of the accessibility statement
