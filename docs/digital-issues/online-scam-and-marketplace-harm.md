# Online Scam, Fake Seller, Or Marketplace Failure

## Typical Problem

You paid for goods or services through a marketplace or platform and encountered fraud, impersonation, non-delivery, fake trader identity, or refusal to correct obvious abuse.

## Save First

- order confirmation
- payment proof
- seller profile and URL
- screenshots of the listing
- platform messages
- delivery timeline
- refund request and answer

## First Route

- use the platform dispute or refund route immediately
- contact payment provider if chargeback or dispute rights may apply
- record whether the trader identity was visible

## Likely Public Routes

- national consumer authority
- police or fraud reporting route if clearly criminal
- Digital Services Coordinator if the platform's trader traceability or notice handling appears defective

## EU Layer

This problem often combines marketplace governance, consumer rights, and payment dispute logic rather than a single EU-only route.

## Escalate When

- the trader is fake or unreachable
- the marketplace ignores repeated evidence
- many consumers are exposed to the same listing pattern

## Useful Ask

Ask for:
- refund or charge reversal
- trader identification information if lawfully available
- copy of the platform's internal decision
