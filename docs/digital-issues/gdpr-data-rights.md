# Data Access, Deletion, Or Profiling Problem

## Typical Problem

You asked for access, deletion, correction, portability, or an explanation of profiling, and the controller ignored, delayed, or inadequately answered.

## Save First

- your original rights request
- proof of submission
- identity verification step, if any
- the controller's response
- date of request and response
- any refusal wording

## First Route

- submit a clear rights request to the controller
- ask for acknowledgement if the form is weak or silent
- keep the request narrow enough to be answerable

## Likely Public Routes

- national data protection authority
- consumer authority if the data issue is wrapped inside a commercial problem

## EU Layer

The legal route is usually national through the competent data protection authority, even when the company is large and cross-border.

## Escalate When

- there is no response
- the answer is generic or incomplete
- profiling materially affected you
- the business claims an exemption without explaining it

## Useful Ask

Ask for:
- full copy of your data
- logic or criteria behind profiling where applicable
- retention periods
- recipients or categories of recipients
- complaint contact for the competent DPA
