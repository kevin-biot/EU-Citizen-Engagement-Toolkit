# Template Email: Commission Consultation Process Challenge

## Suggested Subject

Concern regarding the consultation process for [initiative]

## Template

```text
Dear [Commission service / cabinet / Secretariat-General contact],

I am writing regarding the consultation process for [initiative / call for evidence / omnibus file].

I support the objective of obtaining informed stakeholder input. My concern is that the current process risks under-representing citizens, SMEs, independent experts, and civil-society organisations that do not have the resources to respond at speed across multiple overlapping files.

In particular:
1. the consultation window appears too short for the complexity of the instruments concerned
2. the public documentation does not yet provide a sufficiently consolidated view of scope, sequencing, and next steps
3. the process appears to privilege actors with permanent Brussels capacity over ordinary affected stakeholders

Concrete example:
[one short factual example with date and reference]

I would be grateful if you could clarify:
1. why the current timetable was chosen
2. whether an extension or supplementary consultation round is being considered
3. how the Commission is ensuring balanced stakeholder participation
4. where citizens and smaller organisations can find a consolidated public overview of the initiative

Thank you for your attention.

Yours sincerely,
[name]
[organisation, if any]
[email]
```
