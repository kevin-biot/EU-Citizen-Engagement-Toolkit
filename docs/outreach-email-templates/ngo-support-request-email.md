# Template Email: NGO Support Or Amplification Request

## Suggested Subject

Request for review or support on [issue]

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am contacting you because your organisation works on [topic / rights area], and I believe the issue below may be relevant to your remit.

Short summary:
[two or three sentences on what happened]

Why I think this may matter beyond my individual case:
- [systemic angle]
- [rights, democratic, consumer, or rule-of-law angle]
- [timing or policy relevance]

What I have already done:
1. [complaint / platform appeal / authority filing / consultation response]
2. [follow-up or public record]

What I am asking from you:
- [review the issue]
- [tell me whether it fits your remit]
- [suggest the right route]
- [consider public support or amplification]
- [consider connecting me with the right expert or partner]

Key documents or references:
- [document / URL / filing reference]
- [document / URL / filing reference]

I understand that you may not be able to take on individual cases. Even a brief indication of whether this fits your scope, or where it should go next, would be very helpful.

Best regards,
[name]
[country]
[email]
```
