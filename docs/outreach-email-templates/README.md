# Outreach Email Templates

Short public-interest outreach templates for political pressure, media visibility, and civil-society escalation.

These are not legal documents. They are starting points for emails that sit between a private complaint and a formal filing.

Use these when the goal is to:
- raise a procedural concern with an MEP
- pressure a committee coordinator on pace, transparency, or stakeholder balance
- challenge a Commission consultation process
- pitch a journalist on a public-interest issue
- ask an NGO or network to support, review, or amplify a concern

## Use Them Like This

- keep the ask narrow and explicit
- include one concrete fact, example, or document reference
- explain why the issue matters now
- do not attach a large bundle unless requested
- if you are writing politically, state whether you are a constituent, stakeholder, researcher, business user, journalist, or affected citizen
- if you are writing to media or NGOs, explain what you are asking them to do, not just what happened
