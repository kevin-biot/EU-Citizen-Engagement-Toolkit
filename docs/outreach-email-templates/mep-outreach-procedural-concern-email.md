# Template Email: MEP Outreach On A Policy File Or Procedural Concern

## Suggested Subject

Concern about the process and pace of [policy file]

## Template

```text
Dear [MEP name / office],

I am writing as [a constituent / a French resident / a stakeholder / a citizen who has participated in the consultation process] regarding [policy file].

My concern is not with simplification or reform as such, but with whether this file is being handled in a way that is transparent, balanced, and workable for citizens, SMEs, and non-lobby stakeholders.

I would like to raise three points:

1. [consultation window / drafting pace / public transparency problem]
2. [stakeholder imbalance / access issue]
3. [sequencing or enforcement problem]

One concrete example:
[brief factual example with one date, consultation reference, meeting register entry, or document link]

I would be grateful to know:
1. whether you are satisfied with the current pace and procedural quality of this file
2. whether you see a stakeholder-balance problem
3. what action you or your group intend to take inside Parliament

If useful, I can also send a short note setting out the specific procedural concerns in more detail.

Yours sincerely,
[name]
[postal address]
[email]
```
