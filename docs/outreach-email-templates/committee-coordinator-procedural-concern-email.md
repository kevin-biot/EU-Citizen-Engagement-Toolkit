# Template Email: Committee Coordinator Pressure Email

## Suggested Subject

Procedural concern regarding [file] in [committee]

## Template

```text
Dear [Coordinator name / office],

I am writing regarding [file] and its handling across [committee name] and related committees.

I am addressing this to you because, as [group] coordinator in [committee], you are in a position to say whether the Parliament is satisfied with how the file is being run.

From a citizen and stakeholder perspective, the process currently raises three concerns:

1. the timetable appears too compressed for a technically dense cross-regulation file
2. the public picture of what has been scoped, drafted, or consulted on remains fragmented
3. publicly visible stakeholder access appears uneven, with better-resourced actors better placed to influence the process

Concrete example:
[one short factual example]

I would be grateful if you could indicate:
1. whether your group is satisfied with the procedural quality of the file
2. whether the committee has visibility of any stakeholder-access imbalance
3. what steps you intend to use inside Parliament to improve pace, transparency, or scrutiny

I am not asking you to take a particular substantive position. I am asking whether the process is proportionate to the importance of what is being modified.

Yours sincerely,
[name]
[postal address]
[email]
```
