# Template Email: Journalist Issue Pitch

## Suggested Subject

Possible EU public-interest story: [short angle]

## Template

```text
Hello [name / newsroom],

I am writing in case this is relevant to your coverage of [topic].

I believe there may be a public-interest story in [one-sentence summary].

Why this may matter:
- [why the issue affects citizens / SMEs / rights / public money / democratic process]
- [why the timing matters now]
- [why it is more than an isolated anecdote]

What I have:
- [one or two specific documents, submissions, datasets, consultation references, or public records]
- [one factual example]
- [brief explanation of what I can share on or off the record]

What I am not claiming:
- [brief caution if the evidence is incomplete or still emerging]

If this is of interest, I can send a short factual summary with links and source documents.

Best,
[name]
[contact email]
[phone, if appropriate]
```
