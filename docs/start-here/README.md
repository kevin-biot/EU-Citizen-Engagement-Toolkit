# Start Here

Citizen-first map for people who know something went wrong but do not yet know which institution, route, or pressure path applies.

This section is not the deep toolkit. It is the front door.

Use it if you are thinking things like:

- "I do not know where to start."
- "I do not know whether this is EU, national, or platform."
- "I do not know who actually has power here."
- "I do not know whether to complain, wait, escalate, or go public."
