# What To Do After No Reply

Silence is one of the most common failure modes. It is also where many citizens lose time.

## 1. Check Whether You Made A Real First Contact

Before escalating, ask:

- did I contact the right place
- did I state what happened clearly
- did I keep the confirmation, timestamp, and reference number
- did I ask for something specific

If not, fix that first.

## 2. Separate Silence Into Three Types

### Company Or Platform Silence

Usually means:

- repeat the contact cleanly
- preserve the chronology
- then move to the competent authority if needed

### Authority Or Regulator Silence

Usually means:

- send a procedural follow-up
- then consider pressure only when the record is clear

### Political Office Silence

Usually means:

- the ask may be too weak, too broad, or badly timed
- try a sharper target, better evidence, or a more relevant office

## 3. Do Not Jump To Every Route At Once

That often weakens the case.

Better sequence:

1. make the clean first contact  
2. log silence or refusal  
3. move to the competent authority  
4. only then widen to NGO, journalist, or political pressure if the issue is systemic or stalled
