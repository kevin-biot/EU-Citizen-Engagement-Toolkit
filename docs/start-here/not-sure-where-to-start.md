# Not Sure Where To Start

If you are overwhelmed, do this in order.

## 1. Name The Harm

Write one sentence:

- what happened
- who did it
- when it happened
- what you want to change

Examples:

- "My platform account was suspended without explanation."
- "A company ignored my GDPR access request."
- "A public website is inaccessible."
- "A regulator has ignored my complaint for months."

## 2. Ask Who Actually Has Power

Usually the answer is one of these:

- the company or platform itself
- a national regulator
- an EU institution
- an elected officeholder who can apply pressure
- a journalist or NGO who can widen the issue

If you do not know which one applies, go to [Who Can Move This](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/docs/start-here/who-can-move-this.md).

## 3. Decide Whether This Is A First Contact Or An Escalation

Most people lose time by escalating too early or waiting too long.

Use this rule:

- if you have not yet made a clean documented first contact, do that first
- if you already have a record and there is silence, refusal, or repeat-pattern abuse, move to escalation

If you are stuck here, go to [What To Do After No Reply](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/docs/start-here/what-to-do-after-no-reply.md).

## 4. Pick The Problem Type

Use [First Step By Problem](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/docs/start-here/first-step-by-problem.md) to route quickly.

## 5. Only Then Open The Deeper Layer

Once you know the route class, use the deeper repo material:

- playbooks: [docs/digital-issues](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/docs/digital-issues)
- templates: [docs/digital-issues/email-templates](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/docs/digital-issues/email-templates) and [docs/outreach-email-templates](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/docs/outreach-email-templates)
- campaigns: [campaigns](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/campaigns)
- contacts and authority layers: [data](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/data)

## The Main Trap

Do not start by searching for names.

Start by answering:

- what system failed you
- who has power over that failure
- whether this is first contact or escalation

Names and email addresses matter, but power mapping comes first.
