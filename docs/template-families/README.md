# Template Families

This section explains how the repo's templates are now organized conceptually.

It exists so contributors do not treat every new template as a one-off file.

## Current Families

### Formal Filing

Use for:

- consultation submissions
- access-to-documents requests
- Ombudsman complaints

Characteristics:

- formal structure
- higher evidence expectations
- tighter jurisdiction assumptions

### Citizen Issue Email

Use for:

- first complaints to platforms, traders, service owners, or controllers
- narrow asks tied to a concrete incident

Characteristics:

- short
- citizen-facing
- direct and practical

### Outreach Email

Use for:

- MEPs
- committee coordinators
- Commission process pressure
- journalists
- NGOs

Characteristics:

- public-interest framing
- not a formal filing
- aimed at visibility, review, or political movement

### Campaign Email

Use for:

- escalations inside a live campaign pack
- regulator delay
- coalition coordination
- media pressure on systemic patterns

Characteristics:

- depends on prior chronology
- assumes stronger evidence and sequencing discipline
- often unsuitable as a first move

## Selection Rule

Choose templates by:

1. target
2. stage
3. jurisdiction
4. tone
5. evidence threshold

Do not choose by filename resemblance alone.
