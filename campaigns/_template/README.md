# Campaign Template

Use this as the base structure for any extended issue campaign.

## Core Files

- `README.md`: strategy overview and campaign framing
- `targets.md`: who can move, block, amplify, or validate the issue
- `evidence.md`: what proof is needed and how to organize it
- `messages.md`: message tracks by audience
- `timeline.md`: sequencing and cadence
- `escalation.md`: trigger points and fallback routes
- `missing-data.md`: data gaps and harvest priorities
- `emails/`: reusable outreach drafts
- `press/`: media angles, pitches, and press assets

## Minimum Questions Every Campaign Must Answer

- What is the specific ask?
- What is the scope: local, national, EU, or cross-border?
- Who are the primary targets?
- Who are the secondary amplifiers?
- What evidence must exist before escalation?
- What is the first route, and what are the next three routes if that fails?
- What counts as progress?
- What counts as success?
- What should make us stop, pause, or reframe?
