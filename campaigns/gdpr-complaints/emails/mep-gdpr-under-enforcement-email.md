# Template Email: MEP Oversight Pressure On GDPR Under-Enforcement

## Suggested Subject

Concern regarding GDPR under-enforcement and complaint delay

## Use When

- the issue is clearly systemic
- the concern is no longer only about one controller
- you can explain the enforcement problem in a short factual chronology

## Template

```text
Dear [MEP name / office],

I am writing as a [citizen / resident / constituent] regarding what appears to be a wider GDPR enforcement problem rather than only an individual dispute.

Short summary:
[two or three factual lines]

Why I believe this deserves oversight attention:
- [repeat-pattern or many-users angle]
- [cross-border or one-stop-shop angle]
- [visible delay, silence, or under-enforcement by the authority]
- [public-interest consequence]

What has already happened:
1. rights request to [controller] on [date]
2. complaint to [authority] on [date]
3. follow-up on [date]
4. current status: [brief factual description]

I am not asking you to intervene in a private case decision. I am asking whether you are concerned about the wider enforcement pattern and whether you would consider raising it through the appropriate parliamentary or political channels.

If useful, I can share a short factual chronology and the key public documents.

Yours sincerely,
[name]
[country]
[email]
```
