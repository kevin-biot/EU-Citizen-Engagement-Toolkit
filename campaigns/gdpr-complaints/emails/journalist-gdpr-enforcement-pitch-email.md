# Template Email: Journalist Pitch On GDPR Under-Enforcement

## Suggested Subject

Possible EU public-interest story: GDPR complaint pattern and enforcement delay

## Use When

- the evidence file is coherent
- the issue has wider public-interest value
- the story is bigger than one unresolved personal complaint

## Template

```text
Hello [name / newsroom],

I am writing because I believe there may be a public-interest story in a GDPR enforcement problem that appears to go beyond one individual complaint.

Short summary:
[two or three factual lines on the controller, the practice, and the enforcement problem]

Why this may matter:
- [number of users or wider category affected]
- [cross-border or lead-authority angle]
- [profiling, adtech, surveillance, children, or sensitive-data angle]
- [why the enforcement delay or under-action matters now]

What I have:
- [complaint filing date and authority]
- [timeline of key correspondence or silence]
- [documents, screenshots, public records, or policy materials]
- [what can be shared on the record, off the record, or securely]

What I am not claiming:
- [brief caution where the evidence is incomplete or still emerging]

If this is relevant to your coverage, I can send a short chronology and supporting documents.

Best,
[name]
[country]
[email]
[Signal / phone / secure channel, if appropriate]
```
