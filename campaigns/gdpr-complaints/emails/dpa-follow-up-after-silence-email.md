# Template Email: DPA Follow-Up After Silence

## Suggested Subject

Follow-up on GDPR complaint [reference number or filing date]

## Use When

- a complaint was filed correctly
- the DPA acknowledged or should reasonably have acknowledged it
- there has been silence or no visible progression

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am following up on my GDPR complaint submitted to [authority name] on [date].

Reference number:
[case reference, if one was provided]

Controller or organisation complained about:
[name]

Short issue summary:
[two or three factual lines]

What has happened so far:
1. I submitted the complaint on [date].
2. [I received an acknowledgement on date / I have not yet received an acknowledgement.]
3. [Any further communication or silence.]

I would be grateful if you could confirm:
- whether the complaint is formally on file
- the current procedural status
- whether any further information is needed from me
- the expected next step or timeframe

I am keeping this follow-up factual and limited to the status of the file. If there is a better channel for status follow-up, please let me know.

Best regards,
[name]
[country]
[email]
[phone, if appropriate]
```
