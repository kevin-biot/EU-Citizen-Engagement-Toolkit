# Template Note: Cross-Border GDPR Complaint Coordination

## Suggested Subject

Coordination note on possible cross-border GDPR complaint regarding [controller or practice]

## Use When

- several complainants or partner organisations are comparing the same pattern
- the controller appears to operate across multiple Member States
- one-stop-shop or lead-authority dynamics may matter

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am sharing a short coordination note in case it is useful for others looking at the same GDPR issue.

Controller or organisation:
[name]

Short issue summary:
[two or three factual lines]

Why this may be cross-border:
- [controller operates across several Member States]
- [complainants or affected users are in more than one country]
- [there may be a lead-authority or one-stop-shop dimension]

Known actions so far:
1. [rights request sent by whom, and when]
2. [complaint filed with which authority, and when]
3. [acknowledgement, silence, or procedural update]

Current coordination ask:
- compare timelines
- identify whether the same practice is affecting users in other countries
- avoid sending inconsistent public claims
- decide whether any shared NGO, journalist, or oversight outreach is appropriate

What I can share:
- [chronology]
- [screenshots or documents]
- [public summary / non-public material / secure-channel only]

What I am not claiming at this stage:
- [brief caution on anything still uncertain]

Best,
[name]
[country]
[email or secure channel]
```
