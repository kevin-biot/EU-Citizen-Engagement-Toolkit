# Template Email: Privacy NGO Support Or Amplification Request

## Suggested Subject

Possible strategic GDPR issue: [short angle]

## Use When

- the issue looks systemic rather than purely personal
- a DPA complaint is already filed or nearly ready
- you want validation, support, or amplification rather than private legal representation

## Template

```text
Hello,

I am contacting you because your organisation works on privacy, data protection, and public-interest digital-rights issues, and I believe the matter below may fit that remit.

Short summary:
[two or three factual lines]

Why I think this may matter beyond my individual case:
- [repeat-pattern or many-users angle]
- [cross-border or lead-authority angle]
- [profiling, adtech, surveillance, or automated-decision angle]
- [regulator delay or under-enforcement angle, if relevant]

What I have already done:
1. rights request to [controller] on [date]
2. complaint to [authority] on [date]
3. follow-up or additional evidence on [date]

What I am asking from you:
- tell me whether this fits your remit
- suggest the strongest next route
- consider reviewing the issue for public-interest relevance
- consider support or amplification if you think it is strategic

Key references:
- [complaint reference or filing proof]
- [public document, screenshot set, or case chronology]
- [one short note on what can be shared publicly vs privately]

I understand that you may not take on individual cases. Even a short indication of whether this is strategically relevant, or where it should go next, would be very helpful.

Best regards,
[name]
[country]
[email]
```
