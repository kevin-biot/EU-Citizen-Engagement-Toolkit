# GDPR Complaints Campaign

Extended campaign playbook for GDPR complaints that need more than a basic rights request or a one-off filing.

## Purpose

This campaign pack is for GDPR problems that need structured escalation across controllers, DPAs, NGOs, journalists, and political or public-interest routes.

It is not just for:

- making an access request
- sending a simple deletion request
- filing one complaint and waiting

It is for:

- repeat-pattern controller abuse
- cross-border non-compliance
- regulator delay or under-enforcement
- cases that need evidence discipline plus coalition or media pressure

## Campaign Tracks

### 1. Individual rights complaint

Use when:

- a controller ignored or mishandled access, deletion, correction, portability, or profiling rights
- the immediate goal is a real answer, correction, deletion, or review

Main route:

- rights request to controller
- complaint to competent DPA

### 2. Systemic abuse by one company or platform

Use when:

- the same GDPR failure affects many users
- the issue is linked to consent, profiling, adtech, retention, or dark patterns

Main route:

- complaint package plus external validation from rights groups or public-interest networks

### 3. Cross-border enforcement pressure

Use when:

- the controller is large and cross-border
- one-stop-shop dynamics or lead-authority delay matter

Main route:

- carefully documented complaint
- cross-border framing
- NGO and journalist escalation where appropriate

### 4. Regulator delay or under-enforcement

Use when:

- the DPA is silent, stalled, or visibly under-acting
- the issue is no longer only about the company, but also about enforcement quality

Main route:

- regulator follow-up
- pressure through public-interest organisations, elected representatives, and where appropriate formal maladministration or oversight channels

## Success Conditions

- the controller answers properly or changes practice
- a DPA acknowledges and progresses the case
- a repeat-pattern issue is documented beyond one complainant
- a rights group, journalist, or parliamentary office takes interest where needed
- a regulator-delay narrative is supported by a clean evidence trail rather than frustration alone

## Failure Conditions

- evidence is too thin or disorganized
- too many routes are contacted at once with inconsistent framing
- the ask is unclear
- the campaign mixes personal remedy and public pressure without deciding which comes first
