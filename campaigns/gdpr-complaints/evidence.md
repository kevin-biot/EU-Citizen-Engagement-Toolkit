# GDPR Complaint Evidence

## Minimum Evidence Pack

- original rights request
- proof of submission
- date sent
- date of any acknowledgement
- date of any response
- full controller reply
- screenshots or exports where relevant
- complaint copy as filed with the DPA
- DPA acknowledgement or silence timeline

## Stronger Evidence For Systemic Campaigns

- multiple user examples using the same pattern
- screenshots of consent flows, profiling notices, or retention failures
- product or policy changes over time
- archived versions of relevant pages
- complaint reference numbers across more than one person if available
- records showing regulator delay or contradictory statements

## Evidence Rules

- keep raw timestamps
- preserve original wording
- avoid paraphrasing when the exact refusal matters
- separate fact, inference, and campaign framing
- maintain one master chronology

## What Weakens The Case

- missing dates
- no proof of submission
- quoting only fragments of the controller response
- mixing several legal issues before one is documented properly
- escalating publicly before the basic evidence pack is stable
