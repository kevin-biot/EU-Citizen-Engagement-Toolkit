# Campaigns

Extended campaign playbooks for issues that need more than a one-off complaint or template.

These campaign packs are for situations where you need to coordinate:

- targets
- timing
- message tracks
- evidence
- escalation paths
- coalition outreach
- media pressure
- follow-up cadence

## Structure

- `_template/`: reusable campaign-playbook scaffold
- `gdpr-complaints/`: first live campaign pack

## Design Intent

The campaign layer sits on top of:

- `docs/` for issue playbooks and narrative guidance
- `templates/` for formal submissions
- `data/` for authorities, institutional contacts, bundles, and political routes
- `mcp-server/` for AI-assisted retrieval and drafting

Use campaign packs when the issue is:

- systemic rather than one-off
- multi-target rather than single-recipient
- time-sequenced rather than one-shot
- evidence-heavy
- likely to require public-interest escalation
