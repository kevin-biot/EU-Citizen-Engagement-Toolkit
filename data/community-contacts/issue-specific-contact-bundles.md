# Issue-Specific Contact Bundles

Problem-first routing map for citizens, community organisers, and journalists who need a short list of public-interest contacts by issue type.

Coverage in this pass:
- `6` bundles
- `30` public routes
- mix of EU-level organisations, national digital-rights groups, journalism-support organisations, and EU-facing newsrooms

How to use this file:
- Start with the bundle that matches the problem.
- Use the `why this route` column to choose between rights advocacy, media visibility, emergency journalist support, or consumer/public-interest framing.
- Pair this with [docs/digital-issues/README.md](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/docs/digital-issues/README.md:1) and [data/national-authorities/issue-router.csv](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/data/national-authorities/issue-router.csv:1).

Important:
- These organisations are not interchangeable. Some are advocacy networks, some are watchdogs, some are journalist-support bodies, and some are newsrooms.
- A listed route is not a promise of casework, legal representation, or publication.
