# GDPR Privacy Enforcement Support Routes

Campaign-focused public routes for GDPR complaints that may need watchdog support, secure handling, or newsroom escalation after the core complaint file is in order.

Coverage in this pass:
- `17` public routes
- `16` organisations or outlets
- mix of strategic privacy groups, civil-liberties watchdogs, consumer routes, and public-interest newsrooms

How to use this file:
- Start with the legal and administrative path first: [dpa-complaint-routes.md](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/data/national-authorities/dpa-complaint-routes.md:1).
- Use this file when a GDPR complaint is becoming strategic, cross-border, stalled, sensitive, or media-relevant.
- Pair it with [issue-specific-contact-bundles.md](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/data/community-contacts/issue-specific-contact-bundles.md:1) and [campaigns/gdpr-complaints/escalation.md](/Users/kevinbrown/EU-Citizen-Engagement-Toolkit/campaigns/gdpr-complaints/escalation.md:1).

Important:
- These are public routes, not a promise of legal representation, publication, or emergency response.
- Do not send a half-prepared evidence file to a newsroom.
- Use secure-tip or encrypted routes when the material is sensitive, source-protective, or retaliation risk is real.

| Organization | Scope | Contact | Recommended Use | Notes | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| noyb | `eu_level / general` | `info@noyb.eu` | Use when a GDPR case is legally substantive, repeat-pattern, or clearly cross-border | Public general mailbox published on the official media center page | <https://noyb.eu/en/media-center> |
| noyb | `eu_level / media` | `media@noyb.eu` | Use when a documented GDPR case may support public-interest coverage or needs quote-ready enforcement context | Media route published on the official media center page | <https://noyb.eu/en/media-center> |

| Access Now | `international / help` | `help@accessnow.org` | Use when complainants, journalists, or sources face digital-security risk, retaliation, device compromise, or sensitive evidence handling questions | Digital Security Helpline route; not a substitute for a DPA complaint | <https://www.accessnow.org/help/> |
| BEUC | `eu_level / consumer_route` | `consumers@beuc.eu` | Use when a GDPR problem also looks like consumer deception, unfair design, or a wider market practice | Useful where privacy abuse and consumer-law framing overlap | <https://www.beuc.eu/contact> |
| AlgorithmWatch | `eu_level / general` | `info@algorithmwatch.org` | Use when GDPR issues overlap with profiling, recommender systems, adtech measurement, or automated decisions | Best for public-interest framing rather than personal casework | <https://algorithmwatch.org/en/contact/> |
