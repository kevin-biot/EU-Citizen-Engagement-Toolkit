# National Equality Bodies (Markdown view)

Source: data/national-authorities/national-equality-bodies.csv

This file captures the national equality-body entries listed on the European Commission's official page. It is a routing aid for discrimination and equal-treatment complaints, not a claim that every body has the same mandate or powers.

Coverage in this snapshot: 28 listed body entries across 27 Member States.

Important note: the source page is framed through the Commission's gender-equality section, but it points to national equality bodies more broadly. Some countries have one listed body and some have more than one.
