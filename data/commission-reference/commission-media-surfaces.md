# Commission Media Surfaces (Markdown view)

Source: data/commission-reference/commission-media-surfaces.csv

This file maps the main public-facing communication surfaces around the European Commission for journalists, citizen representatives, NGOs, and researchers. It is not limited to one office: it covers spokespersons, accreditation, audiovisual handling, country press links, and non-media fallback routes.

Notes:

- The Spokespersons' Service page explicitly says it is the official voice of the Commission vis-a-vis the media.
- The same page also tells citizens and professionals not working for media to use general EU contact routes instead.
- Country-level representation press offices are useful when a story needs local-language or national press handling rather than Brussels-first routing.
- Accreditation, audiovisual permissions, and press-room access are distinct surfaces and should not be treated as interchangeable with spokesperson or cabinet outreach.
